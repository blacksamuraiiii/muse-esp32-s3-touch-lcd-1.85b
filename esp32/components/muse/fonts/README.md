# Fonts

Two CJK faces, both built in only with `CONFIG_MUSE_CJK_FONT`, both from GNU
Unifont 16.0.04 so they share the 1-bit look of unscii:

- `muse_font_cjk_16.c` — the caption font's fallback. 16x16 bitmaps, the same
  cell as unscii-16, for CJK punctuation, kana, every CJK Unified Ideograph
  (U+4E00 to U+9FFF) and the fullwidth forms: about 850 KB of flash. Also the
  fallback for the 20 px face, so a rare hanzi still renders. Regenerated by
  `tools/muse/gen_cjk_font.sh`.
- `muse_font_cjk_20.c` — what a reply carrying Chinese is drawn in: 20 px, 1
  bpp, two lines of 12 columns. Coverage is ASCII, CJK punctuation and kana,
  the fullwidth forms and the 6763 GB2312 hanzi — the full ideograph range at
  20 px would be ~1.4 MB and overflow the 4 MB app slot, and what GB2312 lacks
  falls back to the 16 px face. Regenerated by `tools/muse/gen_cjk_font20.sh`.
